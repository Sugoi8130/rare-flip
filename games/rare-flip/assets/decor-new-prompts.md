# New decoration artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, transparent background. Original outputs copied unchanged into this folder. Connected to ROOM shop, preset placement and in-game renderer.

## trophy

File: decor-trophy-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated room decoration for Rare Flip, a classic 16-bit pixel game with a purple-and-gold arcade room. Style: intentional chunky crisp pixel art, limited palette, consistent square pixel grid, subtle stepped 3-tone shading, front view with a small visible top plane (not steep isometric). Strong readable silhouette even when reduced to 50 pixels wide. Centered full object with generous transparent padding. Genuine transparent background, no scene, no UI, no labels, no text, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no ground platform outside the object's own base, no blur, no smooth 3D gradients, no extra props. Subject: a cute compact gold trophy cup with two curled handles, a small cyan jewel on the cup, gold stem, a dark plum stepped pedestal and a small blank ivory plaque. Golden yellow, cream highlights, copper shadows, cyan accent. The cup and handles must look symmetric, polished and readable.
